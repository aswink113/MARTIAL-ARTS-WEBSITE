Admission Process

  1. Application Received: You receive a unique reference number immediately upon submission.
  2. Staff Review & Contact: Our admissions coordinator contacts you within 1–2 working days to confirm space.

  4. Enrolment Confirmation: Admission is formally confirmed once staff approve your placement.
